Krita's scale-aware downscale weights, its own fixed-point arithmetic
from libs/image/kis_filter_weights_buffer.h and kis_filter_strategy.cc
Krita's bilinear strategy is the triangle 1-|t| and IGNORES its scale argument:
the scaling is applied to the POSITION before the weight is evaluated.
