# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be straightforward and be kind. Critique code, not people. Assume the other
person is competent and acting in good faith until you have evidence otherwise.

## Expected behaviour

- Say what you mean plainly. Disagreement is welcome; contempt is not.
- Review the change in front of you. "This breaks X because Y" is useful;
  "this is amateur work" is not.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a change on scope or direction grounds
  without that being a judgement of you.
- Respect the security policy: report vulnerabilities privately, never in a
  public issue.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments, including about someone's
  identity, background, or level of experience.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome personal attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption: reopening settled decisions to relitigate them,
  brigading a thread, or deliberately wasting reviewers' time.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions,
commit messages, and code comments — and in public spaces when representing the
project.

## Enforcement

Report a concern to `conduct@redrob.ai`. Reports are handled confidentially by
the maintainers, who will not be the subject of the report where that is
avoidable.

Maintainers may warn, edit or remove content, or block an account. Actions are
proportionate; a first misstep is normally a private word, not a ban. Where a
report is upheld, the reporter is told what was done.

Maintainers are held to this document rather than exempt from it.
